# Scene plan: "8 señales de que tu mente funciona de forma diferente"

About 2,330 spoken words, roughly **15.5 minutes** at 150 wpm. Sections: intro (~1:40), Parte 1 (~3:20),
Parte 2 (~3:10), Parte 3 (~3:10), Parte 4 (~2:40), conclusión (~1:35). Timing comes from the voice-over
(transcribe → cues 0.1–0.3 s before the keyword).

Format: the approved flat format (`projects/7dias_sin_cenar/`): code infographics plus real drawings shown as
push-in stills. Rules from `youtube-style.md`: picture on every beat, ≤ 6-word captions, ≤ 2 captions on screen, hook cuts
every 1.5–2.5 s. No pose sheets (no exercises). Drawings: **6 scene sheets (2×2 = 24 panels) + 2 item sheets (~20 items)**
≈ 1 drawing per 20 s.

Files: `intro.html`, `parte1.html` … `parte4.html`, `conclusion.html`, shared `common.js`.

## Visual system
- **Progress strip** (top right): 8 small circles with code-drawn icons: 1 magnifier, 2 ear, 3 speech bubble,
  4 hourglass, 5 key, 6 linked bulbs, 7 house/door, 8 apple. The current signal is ringed orange; past ones filled.
- **Signal kicker**: "SEÑAL 3 · DIÁLOGO INTERIOR" caption over the first picture of each signal (≤ 1.5 s, no dark slide).
- **Brain cut-out** (item A1) as the recurring "inside your head" device: regions glow, thought dots, arrows.
- Colours: green = useful side, amber = depends / balance, red = when to worry. Reassurance stamp
  "NO ES UN DIAGNÓSTICO" recurs (intro, P4, conclusion).
- Calm pace in the body (audience 50+): push-ins and slide-ins, no flashes.
